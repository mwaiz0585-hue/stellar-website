About Us

Who We Are

UTP Stellar Astronomy Club is a student organisation dedicated to promoting astronomy, space education, science communication, and community engagement within Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS. We bring students closer to the universe through events, stargazing activities, talks, workshops, outreach programmes, and creative projects.

Purpose

Our Mission

Inspire

To inspire students to explore astronomy, space science, and the beauty of the night sky.

Educate

To organise meaningful events, talks, workshops, and stargazing activities for the UTP community.

Connect

To build a passionate community that connects people through curiosity, discovery, and space.
